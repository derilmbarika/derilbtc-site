Why MTN MoMo Is the Easiest Way to Buy Bitcoin in Cameroon

Most global exchanges do not support FCFA deposits at all. The workarounds people try, like funding a foreign card or wiring money abroad, are slow, expensive and often simply refused: cards issued by Cameroonian banks are regularly declined by international platforms, and bank transfers out of the CEMAC zone come with paperwork and delays that make small purchases pointless.

Mobile Money removes all of that because the cash leg of the trade never leaves Cameroon. You send FCFA from your MTN MoMo balance to a local counterparty, and that counterparty sends Bitcoin to your wallet address over the Bitcoin network. The money moves the way you already pay for everything else, and only the Bitcoin part is international. With most adults in the country already using mobile money daily (see the GSMA State of the Industry report on mobile money), it is the payment rail that actually works here.

That local counterparty is the part you have to get right. It can be a stranger from a Facebook group, which is where most losses happen, or a desk with years of history, public rates and a name behind it. Step by Step: How to Buy Bitcoin with MTN MoMo

  1. Open your Bitcoin wallet and copy your receiving address. In most wallets this is the Receive button on the Bitcoin screen. The address is a long string starting with bc1, 1 or 3. Copy it rather than typing it.
  2. Check the current rate. Our live rates page shows exactly what your FCFA converts to before you commit, with the desk margin already included. No hidden spread appears afterwards.
  3. Message the desk on WhatsApp. Say the amount in FCFA you want to convert and paste your wallet address. You can start the chat here.
  4. Get the quote in writing. We reply with the rate, the exact BTC amount you will receive, and the MoMo number to pay. Read it, ask anything you want, and only continue when the numbers are clear to you.
  5. Send the MoMo payment. Transfer the agreed FCFA from your MTN MoMo or Orange Money wallet to the number confirmed in the chat, and only that number. If anyone gives you a different number mid-conversation, stop.
  6. Receive your Bitcoin. Once the payment lands, we broadcast the BTC to your address and send you the transaction ID in the chat.
  7. Verify it yourself. Paste the transaction ID into any public block explorer and watch the confirmations arrive. You do not have to trust our word for it; the Bitcoin network is the receipt.

What Are the MTN MoMo Limits for Buying Bitcoin?

Your real ceiling is usually your MoMo account limit, not the desk. MTN caps daily and per-transaction transfer amounts based on your registration level, and the caps change from time to time, so check your own limit in the MyMTN app or with MTN directly before a large purchase. When a client wants to buy more than their daily limit allows, our desk simply splits the trade across days or accounts the client controls, confirming each leg in writing. For genuinely large amounts, tell us early: bulk trades get handled with extra verification steps on both sides, which protects you as much as it protects us.

How to Stay Safe When Buying Bitcoin with MoMo

Every week we hear from someone who paid a "trader" found in a Facebook or Telegram group and never received anything. Mobile money transfers are final: once the FCFA lands, only the recipient can send it back. So the safety work happens before you pay, not after. The rules:

  • Never send MoMo to a number you found in a group chat or a comment section. Verified desk numbers only, confirmed inside the official conversation.
  • Check that the MoMo recipient name matches the business or person you think you are paying. "That is my brother's account" is a refusal, not an explanation.
  • Insist on the rate, the BTC amount and the total in writing before you pay. A legitimate desk always agrees; a scammer stalls or pressures you.
  • Never share your wallet recovery phrase with anyone. Buying Bitcoin never requires it, and anyone who asks for it is stealing from you.
  • Start with a small test amount, confirm the BTC arrives, then scale up.
  • Slow down when someone creates urgency. Fake bonuses, expiring rates and "pay in the next five minutes" are pressure tactics, not opportunities.

Buy with money you can leave alone, send to a wallet you control, and keep your own record of the transaction ID. Frequently Asked Questions

Can I buy Bitcoin with MTN MoMo directly, without a desk?
Not directly. MTN does not sell Bitcoin and there is no official MoMo-to-BTC button. You need a counterparty who accepts MoMo and delivers BTC, which is what an OTC desk like DerilBTC does. A verifiable desk is faster and far safer than trading with strangers found online.
What is the minimum amount I can buy?
We handle everything from small first purchases of about 10,000 FCFA up to large transactions. For bigger amounts we split delivery for safety and confirm each leg in writing before it moves.
How long does it take to receive my Bitcoin?
Typically minutes after your MoMo payment confirms. Bitcoin network congestion can add time on-chain, which is why we always share the transaction ID so you can track the confirmations yourself on a public block explorer.
Does Orange Money work as well as MTN MoMo?
Yes. The process is identical with Orange Money: confirm the rate and total in writing, send to the verified number, receive BTC to your wallet with a transaction ID. Use whichever wallet already holds your money.
Is buying Bitcoin with MoMo legal in Cameroon?
Owning crypto is not criminalized for individuals, but the CEMAC and COBAC regulatory environment is restrictive for institutions and still evolving. Buy from a transparent desk, keep records of your transactions, and never let anyone else hold your coins for you.
What if the Bitcoin price changes while I am paying?
The rate we confirm in writing is held for the short window it takes you to send the MoMo payment. If you delay for hours, we simply re-quote at the current rate before you pay. You are never charged a surprise difference after the fact.
Can I buy USDT with MoMo instead of Bitcoin?
Yes, and many clients do exactly that when they want stable value rather than exposure to Bitcoin's price. The steps are the same: agree the rate on WhatsApp, pay by MoMo, receive USDT to your wallet address in minutes.
