## How does buying from an OTC desk work?

An OTC desk is a broker you deal with directly, usually over WhatsApp. You say how much FCFA you want to convert, the desk quotes you a fixed rate, you pay by MoMo, Orange Money or bank transfer, and the desk sends bitcoin straight to your wallet address.

Three things make this the default choice for most people we serve:

- **The rate is locked before you pay.** No slippage between payment and delivery, which matters when the market moves fast.
- **One conversation, real accountability.** A desk that has operated publicly for years, under a real name, cannot vanish on one trade without destroying its business.
- **It works with the payment methods Cameroonians actually have.** No Visa card requirement, no SWIFT wire, no waiting on a foreign bank.

The catch: the entire model rests on the desk being honest. A fake "desk" on WhatsApp looks identical to a real one until your money is gone. ## Is Binance P2P still worth it in Cameroon?

P2P marketplaces match you with an individual seller and hold the crypto in escrow until you confirm payment. On paper that solves trust. In practice, our desk spends part of every week helping people recover from P2P trades that went sideways.

The recurring problems we see:

1. **Third-party payment scams.** A seller asks you to receive money into your MoMo from a stranger, and your line gets flagged when that money turns out to be stolen. Some clients have had numbers blocked for weeks.
2. **Payment released, coins never confirmed.** Timing games around the escrow window catch beginners constantly.
3. **Thin weekend liquidity.** The advertised "best price" often sits behind sellers with high minimums or bad completion rates, and the realistic price is worse than a desk quote.

P2P can work if you are experienced, patient and trading small amounts. If you use it, never trade outside escrow and never accept payments from accounts that do not match the counterparty's verified name. We cover the MoMo side of these traps in our [MoMo scams guide](/momo-scams-cameroon/).

## Why do international exchanges rarely work from Cameroon?

Coinbase, Kraken and similar platforms are built around funding rails Cameroon does not reliably have: local card processing, SEPA, ACH or fast bank integrations. As of July 2026, most CEMAC-issued cards fail on deposit, and the accounts that do open often stall at verification. Banks in the CEMAC zone remain restricted from servicing crypto businesses under the regional regulator's cautious stance, which is why direct bank funding is effectively unavailable. That restriction is supervisory, not criminal: we explain the difference in [is bitcoin legal in Cameroon](/blog/is-bitcoin-legal-in-cameroon/).

Where exchanges genuinely shine is as a research and price-reference layer. Sites like [CoinGecko](https://www.coingecko.com/en/coins/bitcoin) show the global BTC price in real time, which you should always know before accepting any local quote. The gap between the global price and your local quote is the spread, and understanding it is the single best negotiating tool you have.

## What about street agents and voucher swaps?

Cash agents exist in Douala and Yaounde, often working from phone shops or forex corners. Some are honest. The structural problem is that nothing about the trade is verifiable: no fixed identity, no trade history, no recourse. The spreads we hear from clients who used agents are consistently the widest in the market, and disputes end with nobody to call. Our breakdown of [where traders actually buy bitcoin in Douala](/blog/buy-bitcoin-douala/) shows how much wider those counter prices run in practice, and why meeting anyone in person with cash is the riskiest version of this trade.

Voucher routes (buying a gift card or prepaid voucher, then swapping it for bitcoin) stack two conversions on top of each other, and you lose margin on both. If you already hold gift cards, [selling them directly for FCFA](/sell-gift-cards-cameroon/) almost always beats converting them into crypto first.

## FAQ

### What is the cheapest way to buy bitcoin in Cameroon?

For most amounts, a reputable OTC desk gives the best all-in cost because the quoted rate is final and there are no hidden platform, card or withdrawal fees. P2P can occasionally beat it on paper, but after failed trades, time lost and risk, the desk quote usually wins in practice.

### Can I buy bitcoin in Cameroon with MTN MoMo or Orange Money?

Yes. Mobile money is the most common payment method for bitcoin purchases in Cameroon. You send MoMo or Orange Money to the desk or seller, and the bitcoin is delivered to your wallet address, typically within minutes at a desk.

### How much bitcoin can I buy at once?

The practical ceiling is usually your payment rail, not the seller. MTN MoMo and Orange Money have daily transfer limits, so larger trades are split across mobile money and bank transfer. A good desk will structure this with you before you send anything.

### Is it safe to buy bitcoin from a WhatsApp trader?

It is safe only if the trader is verifiable: years of public history, a real identity, consistent reviews and a business that would lose everything by cheating one client. It is unsafe when the profile is fresh, the rate looks too good and pressure is high. Verify first, trade second.

### Do I need my own wallet before buying?

Yes, ideally. Buying into your own wallet (like Trust Wallet or a hardware wallet) means you control the coins from minute one. A desk can guide you through wallet setup in a few minutes if you are starting from zero.
